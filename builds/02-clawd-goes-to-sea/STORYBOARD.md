# Storyboard — Clawd Goes to Sea

One visual joke for each lyric line. Times are song seconds (see `assets/timing.js` for word-level times).
Each line lasts 4.8 s. "SFX" is the comic sound word in big yellow letters (`Kit.sfx`).
A transition covers each part boundary for 1.1 s (0.55 s each side); the style is fixed by the core.

## Part 1 — Signing On (0.0–28.8 s, includes the 9.6 s intro) — harbor, morning
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–9.6 | (fiddle intro) | A wooden sign swings on the dock: "CLAWD GOES TO SEA" painted on it. The ship bobs at the pier. Clawd walks in from the left, tiny legs a blur. | TOOT! (ship horn puff) |
| 9.6 | Oh, Clawd was a block of the brightest orange hue | Clawd glows so bright that a gull on a mooring post slides on tiny sunglasses as he passes. | BLING! |
| 14.4 | With two dot eyes and stubby legs that barely grew | A thin rope lies across the dock. Clawd's legs are too short to step over it, so he hops it like a bouncing box. | BOING! |
| 19.2 | He marched to the docks with a scientist small | The small scientist marches behind in step, carrying a wobbling tower of lab flasks twice their height. | CLINK! |
| 24.0 | In a white lab coat, and they signed on with a scrawl | The captain holds out the crew book. Clawd signs by stamping his whole body on the page: a perfect orange square. The scientist writes a formula that runs off the page. Both hop aboard as the ship leaves. | STAMP! |

Transition to part 2: a painted wave rolls across the screen.

## Part 2 — Heave Away (28.8–48.0 s, chorus) — open sea, sunny, into sunset
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28.8 | So heave away, Clawd, and haul away, crew | The crew hauls a rope in a line. Clawd holds the end; on each heave he is flung up in the air like a yo-yo. | HEAVE! / HO! |
| 33.6 | The scientist counts every wave as it grew | The scientist chalks a tally for each wave on a board. The waves get bigger with every mark; the last one soaks the scientist. | SPLOOSH! |
| 38.4 | With a stomp on the deck and a fiddle's cry | Every stomp makes the whole ship hop out of the water. The sailor's fiddle has a face and cries big cartoon tears as it is played. | STOMP! |
| 43.2 | We'll sail till the orange sun drops from the sky | The sunset sun turns orange and square, like Clawd. Clawd waves at it. It drops into the sea with a plunk. | PLONK! |

Transition to part 3: a painted wave rolls across the screen.

## Part 3 — Learning the Ropes (48.0–67.2 s) — on deck, bright morning
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 48.0 | They taught him the knots but he tied up his feet | A sailor shows a neat knot. Clawd copies it and ends with all four legs tied in a bow, then tips over like a gift box. | THUD! |
| 52.8 | He swabbed the whole deck till it squeaked clean and neat | Clawd mops at full speed. The deck shines so much that the scientist skates across it like on ice, with sparkle stars. | SQUEAK! |
| 57.6 | The scientist tested the salt of the sea | The scientist lowers a pipette on a fishing line and tastes the drop. The scientist and a fish that also tasted it pucker together. | PTOOEY! |
| 62.4 | And wrote in the notebook: Confirmed! Salty! Whee! | Close-up of the notebook: "Confirmed! Salty! Whee!" with a bar chart. The scientist spins with joy; the fish holds up a sign: "Duh." Dark clouds appear on the horizon. | WHEE! |

Transition to part 4: storm clouds roll down from the top.

## Part 4 — The Storm (67.2–86.4 s) — black sky, tall waves. Thunder at 67.7 s and 78.0 s
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 67.2 | Then the sky went black and the thunder rolled | A grumpy cloud pulls the sky down like a window blind. The thunder "rolls": a big grey barrel rolls across the cloud tops. | KRA-KOOM! (at 67.7) |
| 72.0 | The waves grew tall and the wind blew cold | A wave grows taller than the mast and grins. Clawd shivers and turns blue for a moment, with icicles on his nubs. | BRRR! |
| 76.8 | Clawd held the wheel with his stubby legs braced | Clawd grips the wheel, legs braced, but he stands on a stack of the scientist's books to reach it. A gust spins the wheel and Clawd spins with it. | CRACK! (at 78.0) |
| 81.6 | While the scientist's hair stood straight up in place | Lightning hits the mast. The scientist's hair stands straight up and glows; the light bulb in the scientist's hand lights up. | ZAP! |

Transition to part 5: a lightning white-out.

## Part 5 — Heave Through the Gale (86.4–105.6 s, storm chorus) — heavy rain. Thunder at 86.6 s and 101.2 s
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 86.4 | So heave away, Clawd, through the wind and the rain | The crew heaves in the rain. Clawd's rope is tied to a rain cloud and he pulls it down like a balloon; it rains only on him. | WHOOSH! |
| 91.2 | The scientist spins like a weather vane | The scientist stands on the mast top with one arm out and spins with each gust. N, E, S, W letters circle around. | SPIN! |
| 96.0 | With a stomp on the deck and a mighty ho | The crew stomps once, all together. The ship jumps clean over a wave. | HO! |
| 100.8 | We'll ride out the storm wherever we go | The ship surfs down a giant wave. Clawd stands on the bow in a surfer pose; the scientist holds the mast and flaps like a flag. | KA-BOOM! (at 101.2) |

Transition to part 6: the sun bursts through (golden disc).

## Part 6 — Land Ho (105.6–124.8 s) — clouds break, blue sky. Gull cry at 110.8 s
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 105.6 | Then the clouds broke up and the sun peeked through | The clouds break apart like puzzle pieces. The sun peeks through the gap with a face and sunglasses. | PEEK-A-BOO! |
| 110.4 | A gull flew by in a sky gone blue | A gull lands on Clawd's flat head as if it is a helipad; little runway lights blink on Clawd's top edge. | SQUAWK! (at 110.8) |
| 115.2 | The scientist squinted through a telescope long | The telescope extends and extends, across the whole screen, until its end reaches the island. A crab on the island looks back into it. | ZOOOM! |
| 120.0 | And shouted: Land ho! and the crew burst into song | The shout blows off the crew's hats. Music notes burst out of everyone, and out of the gull and a fish too. | LAND HO! |

Transition to part 7: a painted wave rolls across the screen.

## Part 7 — Homeward (124.8–150.0 s, final chorus + outro) — island dock, celebration
| Time | Lyric | Visual joke | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| 124.8 | So heave away, Clawd, there's land on the lee | Clawd heaves the anchor overboard: it is a tiny anchor. Then he jumps onto the sand and leaves square footprints. | PLOP! |
| 129.6 | A block and a scientist, bold as can be | Hero pose on a rock: the lab coat billows like a cape, and a square medal shines on Clawd. | TA-DA! |
| 134.4 | With a stomp on the dock and a fiddle's cheer | Everyone stomps on the dock. At each stomp a coconut drops from the palm onto the scientist's head. The fiddle with a face cheers with happy tears. | STOMP! |
| 139.2 | We'll sing this shanty for many a year | Time-lapse: the palm grows, the scientist grows a long white beard. Clawd does not change at all (blocks do not age); he just gets a tiny cane. | — |
| 144.0–150.0 | Stomp! Stomp! HEY! | Two big stomps, then everyone jumps on "HEY!" (145.2 s). The sail shows "THE END". Clawd winks. | HEY! |
